**Part 1: How it routes tokens to experts**
*   *Concept:* Standard Transformer (like GPT-3 or Llama) has one big matrix multiplication (Attention + FFN) for every token. MoE replaces the FFN (Feed-Forward Network) with a set of smaller, specialized FFNs (experts).
*   *Routing Mechanism:*
    *   There's a "Gating Network" (usually a small linear layer).
    *   It takes the input token embeddings (or hidden states).
    *   It outputs a probability distribution over the available experts (e.g., 8 experts, 5 active per token).
    *   Top-k selection: For each token, the top $k$ experts (e.g., top-2) are selected based on the highest probabilities.
    *   Load Balancing: A small loss term is added to the training objective to ensure all experts get used roughly equally, preventing dead experts.
    *   Output: The selected tokens are passed to the selected experts, and the outputs are combined (usually a weighted sum) to form the final hidden state.

**Part 2: Why it saves compute**
*   *Standard Transformer Cost:* $O(L \times d^2)$ roughly, where $L$ is sequence length and $d$ is hidden dimension. The FFN is the heavy lifter.
*   *MoE Cost:*
    *   The FFN is replaced by $N$ smaller FFNs, where $N$ is the number of experts, and each expert has dimension $d/N$ (roughly).
